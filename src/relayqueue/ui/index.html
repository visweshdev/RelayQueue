The same API powers this workspace and your applications.

Submit a job

Send your workspace key in the X-API-Key header. Use your assigned queue.

POST /v1/jobs
X-API-Key: YOUR_WORKSPACE_KEY
Content-Type: application/json

{"task":"noop","queue":"YOUR_QUEUE"}

Follow its progress

GET /v1/jobs/JOB_ID
X-API-Key: YOUR_WORKSPACE_KEY

The response includes status, result, and attempt history. Retry dead jobs with POST /v1/jobs/JOB_ID/retry, or request cancellation with POST /v1/jobs/JOB_ID/cancel.

Jobs execute at least once. Handlers should tolerate retries. Cancellation cannot undo completed external effects.
